# Editor surface

Scope: the whole app (index.html). Visitor mode: Operate. The owner edits one photo at a time, mostly on an iPhone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The photo is the only color on screen. Refuses the dark "creative tool" shell with neon accents, glass docks and rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm concrete gray stage (#d4d3d0), off-white paper panels (#f3f2ef), black ink as the only accent. Square corners, 1px hairlines, uppercase tracked grotesk labels, Times-style serif for hints. Chosen by the owner after pointing to Rick Owens, SSENSE and Figma.

STORY: Open a photo, pick a recipe, adjust the stack in running order, export.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a phone, one-line header (Open, AFTERIMAGE wordmark, Export), then the photo floating on gray with a thin text toolbar underneath.

SIGNATURE: While the image is rendering, a hairline runs along the top edge of the stage instead of a glowing status dot.

RISK: Light UI makes dark photos look darker; mid-gray stage mitigates.
